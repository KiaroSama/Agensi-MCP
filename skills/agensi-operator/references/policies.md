Checked **2026-10-12** against [Terms](https://www.agensi.io/terms), [Stripe Terms](https://www.agensi.io/stripe-terms), [Privacy](https://www.agensi.io/privacy), [MCP](https://www.agensi.io/mcp), [Pricing](https://www.agensi.io/pricing) and [Sell](https://www.agensi.io/sell). Legal pages now show October12 updates. Refresh the applicable offer before consequential actions; this is operating guidance, not legal advice or a guarantee of provider decisions.

## Access, not buyer downloads

Free and owned one-time skills load the latest approved instructions through the connected agent on each use. Buyers cannot download, save, copy, share or export acquired skill contents; paid content carries a personal order-linked licence. One-time ownership remains personal, non-transferable access, not an offline ZIP or a promise of perpetual hosting. Creators retain their original work and may download their own content.

Preserve licence notices; use public metadata for comparison and write original methods. Neither free pricing nor access ownership establishes open-source/team/resale rights. Do not reproduce acquired instructions in generated outputs, examples or products. Existing local files are not automatically disposable; this policy update does not authorize deletion or redistribution.

This project's original internal operator plugin and original authoring files are distinct from acquired marketplace content. They can remain local under their own licence and explicit user authorization.

## Dollars, payments and expiry

- **1 credit = $1**. Fractional dollar balances are not the former integer units. Existing balances were converted at full value; the announced example is 100 old credits → $20, not $100. This is policy/example, not evidence of this user's actual converted balance.
- One-off credit top-ups start at $5. The observed `buy_credits` schema accepts $5–$1000 and returns a card checkout link; the user chooses the amount and completes payment. No agent-side charge or automatic top-up.
- Paid one-time skills start at **$1**. Below $5, payment is credit only; at $5 or above, card or credit is available. No purchase without approval for exact skill, current price and payment route. Insufficient credit means a genuine top-up offer, not an overdraft or hidden fallback.
- Credit is valid for **12 months** from purchase. Converted balances retain their original expiry; current Terms separately assign old Starter/Pro plan credits an October12,2027 expiry. Do not invent an account-specific expiry. Credit expiry does not expire already purchased one-time access.
- **Starter and Pro** credit plans are no longer offered; credit is a one-off top-up. Enterprise contact-based offers remain separate. The retired free monthly trial allowance is not a purchase/testing strategy; free skills still exist.

**Individual subscription skills remain a current Terms exception:** monthly card billing, auto-renewal, cancellation with access until the paid month ends, and instructions protected on Agensi's servers. Credits cannot buy these subscriptions. Do not interpret removal of credit plans as removal of every subscription or promise local subscription instructions. Verify the actual listing and entitlement before any authorized server-side execution.

## Refunds

Current Terms and Stripe Terms agree: contact info@agensi.io within **30 days** if the skill does not do what its listing promises. The platform describes a full refund, subject to its abuse exception; card refunds go to the original payment method, credit refunds to balance, and access is removed. This supersedes earlier 14-day/“any reason” guidance. Do not claim a refund was issued from the policy alone.

Unused top-up credit has a separate 30-day refund policy less spent credit. Use a sanitized transaction reference through the approved provider channel; never send credentials or full local logs. Do not apply one-time refund terms to a subscription without its applicable terms.

## One connected agent and privacy

**One connected agent** at a time: signing in through another agent disconnects the previous one. OAuth connector sign-in, not invented API keys. Do not silently start another authentication, share/reuse tokens to evade entitlement, or promise Desktop and Code can both remain independently authenticated. A bridge's local transport is not a multi-agent licence.

“In-session/no download” is a delivery rule, **not a zero-persistence guarantee**. Host chat history, transcripts, caches, telemetry, crash reporting and the AI provider can persist tool results under their policies. This bridge does not log protocol bodies, but that does not audit every client or provider. Review host persistence before loading protected instructions; no verified zero-data-retention or training-use exclusion is established here.

Privacy describes connection/client identity, loaded skill/version, searches, purchases and usage timestamps; usage records2years and financial/order/licence records7years. First use may share the user's display name with the creator, not email/contact. It lists Supabase, Stripe, Resend, Zoneless and Solana RPC. Subscription task-input/output retention and actual inference-provider guarantees remain incomplete. A skill can introduce additional external APIs/costs; approval to load is not permission to upload private data.

## Creators and economics

Creators retain IP, provide original/distributable rights and truthful safe packages, and grant Agensi its stated non-exclusive platform delivery/display licence. Every listing is agent-accessible; buyer downloads are no longer the delivery model. Paid minimum is $1; under-$5 credit-only access applies to buyers.

Creator share remains **70%** of net excluding applicable tax, platform30%, with processing included, whether one-time buyers pay by card or credit; Terms also cover subscription/bundle transactions and refund reversals. Actual earnings require actual transactions, realized prices, dates, refunds and tax. Public installs/“Downloads” labels are not sales, successful runs or revenue; never multiply counters by price and70%.

Paid readiness requires provider-supported payout evidence. Main Terms permits enabled Stripe or Zoneless USDC; Stripe Terms still says Stripe is needed to sell. This payout discrepancy remains unresolved; current account/provider onboarding controls. Never request wallet keys, seed phrases or card credentials. Review estimates24–48h are not guarantees; submitted, approved and live remain distinct.

## Schema and safety boundaries

The October12 announcement retires free runs. Live tool discovery still includes `my_free_runs`, old `run_skill` trial wording and old `check_updates` file-write wording. Do not treat that stale metadata as permission or claim backend methods/trial enforcement were removed. Fresh loading and current licensing control buyer use; inspect actual schemas/results without experimenting with payments or trials.

The public tool registry is a marketing subset, not an exhaustive schema. Free anonymous preview versus account-to-use wording remains ambiguous. Main Terms' general credit availability is narrower for subscription skills under its explicit exception. Privacy's “no third-party analytics” wording and publicly referenced analytics scripts differ; script presence is not proof of actual transmission.

A listing scan/pass is not safety, accuracy or admin approval. Inspect source/capabilities, input handling and permission boundaries. Provider advice to pipe acquired scripts is not authorization to execute unreviewed code. [Agent Skills](https://agentskills.io/specification) defines format; [Claude Code skills](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/skills) defines its loader. `allowed-tools` is preapproval, not a restrictive sandbox. User and host authorization always apply.
